Privacy Policy

Fly2South · Last updated: October 2026

Your privacy matters. This policy explains what information Fly2South (the “app”) does and does not handle. The short version: the app collects no personal data. The only thing that ever leaves your device is an approximate location for the optional Birds Near Me feature, sent to eBird when you ask for it.

Information we collect

None. Fly2South does not collect, sell, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-ups, and no advertising or analytics services. The one exception to “nothing leaves your device” is the approximate location used by Birds Near Me, described below.

Data stored on your device

The app saves your game progress and preferences — such as journeys in progress, best scores, achievements, the daily-challenge streak, and settings — locally on your device only. This information never leaves your device, is not accessible to us, and is removed if you delete the app.

Maps

The app uses Apple’s MapKit to display migration routes. Map imagery is provided by Apple. Apple’s handling of map services is governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Birds Near Me (optional)

Birds Near Me is the only feature that uses your location, and only when you tap Find birds near me and grant permission. Your position is rounded to roughly 1 km and sent to eBird (the Cornell Lab of Ornithology) solely to look up birds recently reported nearby. If nothing has been reported nearby, the app uses Apple’s geocoding service to work out which country you’re in and shows birds seen across that country instead. Your location is not stored by the app or sent to us. eBird’s handling of requests is governed by the Cornell Lab’s Privacy Policy.

Bird photos on collection cards are loaded from Wikipedia using only the bird’s scientific name. No information about you is included. The cards you collect are stored on your device only.

Notifications

If you turn on weather alerts, the app may show local notifications. These are scheduled and delivered entirely on your device with your permission — no notification data is sent to us or any third party. You can disable them at any time in the app’s settings or in iOS Settings.

Children’s privacy

Fly2South is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
